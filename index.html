WORDS FROM THE HEART

Dear Love,

♡

My love, Rheyn,

I don't know if these words will ever be enough to explain everything I feel inside, but I want to try. This is not about pretending to be a perfect man. This is about being honest with you, admitting my imperfections, and letting you see the person I am trying so hard to become.

I know I'm not perfect. I make mistakes. Sometimes I fall short of the person I want to be, and there may be moments when my actions don't reflect the love I have in my heart. I know that loving you isn't just about saying the right words. It is about showing you respect, understanding your feelings, listening when you need me, and taking responsibility when I get things wrong.

For the times I've hurt you, disappointed you, or made you feel less loved than you deserve, I'm truly sorry. I don't want to use my imperfections as an excuse. I want to recognize where I need to grow and put real effort into becoming better.

Rheyn, you are not just my partner. You are someone with your own dreams, feelings, struggles, and hopes. You deserve to feel heard, respected, appreciated, and loved not only when things are easy, but especially when life becomes difficult.

And then there is our Zinoah. ♡

Being Zinoah's father is one of the most meaningful parts of my life. I want to become the kind of father who makes our child feel safe, loved, supported, and proud. I want Zinoah to grow up seeing that being a good man doesn't mean never making mistakes. It means admitting them, learning from them, treating people with kindness, and having the courage to change.

I want to become a better father and a better partner, not only because I want our family to be happy, but because both of you deserve to experience the best of me. I know that love alone is not enough if I don't put that love into action.

I'm still learning, Rheyn. I'm still figuring things out. There are parts of myself that I need to improve, habits I need to change, and lessons I need to take seriously. I won't pretend that changing will always be easy, and I won't promise that I'll suddenly become perfect.

But I want you to know that I'm trying my best. I want to be more patient, more understanding, more responsible, and more thoughtful. I want to learn how to listen without becoming defensive, how to understand before reacting, and how to show love in ways that make you feel it.

I want my actions to become the proof of the promises I make. I want to keep growing even when nobody is watching. I want to become someone who doesn't simply apologize after making a mistake, but someone who learns how to avoid repeating it.

When I think about our future, I think about the little things that make a family meaningful: the ordinary days, the laughter, the challenges we overcome, the lessons we learn, and the memories we create together. I know our journey won't always be perfect, but I hope to contribute to a home where love is shown through patience, honesty, respect, and effort.

I cannot change yesterday. I cannot erase every hurt or take back every mistake. But I can decide what kind of man I want to be tomorrow. And I want to keep making that decision with the future of our family in mind.

I'm not asking you to forget your feelings or pretend that everything is okay. You deserve the time and space to feel what you feel. I don't expect one letter to fix everything. I know that trust and healing take more than beautiful words.

I just hope you can see that beneath all my imperfections is a man who recognizes that he still has a lot to learn, a father who wants to do right by his child, and a partner who wants to love more wisely and responsibly.

Rheyn, I want to keep growing. I want to keep learning. I want to keep becoming someone whose presence brings peace instead of pain, whose words can be trusted, and whose actions show that his family matters.

I may not be the perfect man today, but I don't want to stop trying to become a better one.

For you, Rheyn. For our Zinoah. And for the person I know I can still become.

I'm sorry for my shortcomings. Thank you for the moments we've shared and for the meaning you both bring to my life. I want to carry these lessons with me and let my growth speak for itself.

I'm not perfect, my love. But I'm trying my best. ♡

With love and a sincere heart,
Your Love.
